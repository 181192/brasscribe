First screens for Fretscribe: tab from any recording, for guitar, bass, ukulele and mandolin. These are the three screens that differ most from Brasscribe, in the Ruled Paper brand. Switch your system to dark mode to see the dark palette.

Settle the song, then practise it

The example is an E♭-tuned riff. Fretscribe asks about the tuning before showing any tab, because one wrong answer there makes every fret wrong.

Five signals in the tab, each with its own shape

Colour only reinforces. Each signal has a shape, so none of them depends on its hue. Red never appears in the tab, because it can look like the doubt colour with colour-blindness.

Doubtful pitch

A "?" above the column, the numeral in umber on a faint wash. Only notes below the threshold show it.

No valid place

A boxed "!" in ink. Usually the tuning or instrument is wrong, so it links to Change tuning.

Playback cursor

A 3 px line in blue ink over the current beat's column.

Repeat

A band with brackets at both ends and the words "Repeat 12–13".

Selection

A box in ink on a neutral wash. Focus is an ink ring, never a signal colour.

Numbers you can read from a music stand

Fret numbers use Fretscribe Tab: Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono at weight 600 with a plain zero built in, so every renderer shows it without font features. Titles use Atkinson Hyperlegible Next; everything else uses the platform's own font.

The whole flow

Check the song comes before any note is shown. Practice is where a song opens every time after that.

flowchart LR
  H[Home: Record, Open a recording, Your songs] --> W[What is this? Just my instrument or a full song]
  W --> T[Writing down the notes]
  T --> SC[Check the song: tuning, capo, octave, key]
  SC --> R[Check the notes: pitch first]
  R --> P[Practice: tab, repeat, speed]
  P <--> F[Fix a note: pitch or place]
  P --> X[Share or print]
  H -->|open a song| P
