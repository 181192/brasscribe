Nothing was heard

The recording is silent. This usually means one of these:

  • The music app blocks recording. Streaming apps usually do.
  • Nothing was playing while you recorded.
A file you own, or a recording with the microphone, always works.

Can't reach Brasscribe on your computer

Your recording is safe. Full-band scores are made on your computer, and it isn't answering.

  1. Check that the computer is on and on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Open Brasscribe on the computer.
  3. Choose Try again.
Details for the band's tech person

Error C-2: no answer from 192.168.1.20:8765. On the computer, run brasscribe serve --host 0.0.0.0 and check that the firewall allows port 8765.
