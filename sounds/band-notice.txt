Brasscribe band sounds

The band SoundFont is built from:

- VSCO 2 Community Edition, Versilian Studios LLC (CC0 1.0): brass (trumpet, trumpet vibrato,
  F horn, tenor trombone, tuba) and concert percussion (bass drum, snare, clash cymbals).
  https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
- University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples, Electronic Music Studios, Lawrence Fritts
  ("may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions"): trumpet, horn,
  tenor and bass trombone, tuba. https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
- MuseScore MS Basic SoundFont (MIT): drum kit (hi-hats, toms, ride, hand percussion) and the
