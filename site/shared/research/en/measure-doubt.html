• Precision and recall of the confidence marks

    A note whose estimated probability of being correct falls below a threshold is marked ?question mark. Let W be the written notes that are wrong (unmatched under {{ref eq:prf}}) and Q the marked notes:

    P? = |Q ∩ W| / |Q|,   R? = |Q ∩ W| / |W| {{label eq:marks}}

    The false-alarm rate is the share of correct notes that are marked. In the drawing three notes are wrong and two are marked, one of them correctly: R? = 1/3, P? = 1/2.
