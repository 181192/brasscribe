• Written F-measure

    The strictest measure combines pitch, onset and notation. It is the F-measure of {{ref eq:prf}} at a ±100 ms onset tolerance, with a matched note counted as a true positive only if its position in the bar also equals the score's:

    TPw = |{matched i : position in the bar correct}|,   Fw from TPw as in {{ref eq:prf}} {{label eq:written}}

    Metre, bar phase and the quantisation grid must therefore all be correct for a note to count.
