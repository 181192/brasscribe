• Note-level precision, recall and F-measure

    Notes are evaluated as in mir_eval {{cite raffel2014mireval}}, which implements the MIREX note-tracking convention {{cite bay2009mirex}}. An estimated note matches a reference note when its onset lies within ±50 ms of the reference onset and its pitch within ±50 cents; each note is matched at most once. With TP matched notes, FP unmatched estimated notes and FN unmatched reference notes:

    P = TP / (TP + FP),   R = TP / (TP + FN),   F = 2PR / (P + R) {{label eq:prf}}

    Offsets are ignored unless stated. Where they are used (onset+offset F-measure), the offset must also lie within max(50 ms, 20 % of the reference note's duration). We also report F with a ±100 ms onset tolerance, and say so wherever it is used: softly attacked brass notes are detected late relative to their annotated onsets, and deviations of that size are absorbed when the notes are quantised.
