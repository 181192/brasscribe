• Onset F1 and its tolerance

    A written note is right when a played note has the same pitch and starts within the tolerance: 50 ms is the usual choice, and we also report 100 ms because a soft brass attack speaks late. Precision is the share of the written notes that are right, recall the share of the played notes that were found, and F1 combines the two. Note ends are ignored unless a figure says otherwise.

    F1 = 2 · precision · recall / (precision + recall)
