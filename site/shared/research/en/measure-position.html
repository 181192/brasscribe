• Position and duration accuracy

    Rhythm is scored on the N notes that have a position in the reference score. Let qi be the quantised position of note i (in ticks, 24 per beat) and si its notated position, after one global shift by a whole number of beats:

    Abar = |{i : qi = si}| / N {{label eq:position}}

    Position within the beat, Abeat, compares the positions modulo one beat, so it is unaffected by a displaced bar line. Duration accuracy is the share of notes whose written duration equals the notated one.
