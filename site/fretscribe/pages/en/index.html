Turn a recording into tab.

Play a riff, record a song or open a file you have. Fretscribe writes it down as tab for your instrument and your tuning, shows you what it heard before a single fret is written, and lets you practise with the recording.

    Speed slows the recording down, Repeat bars plays the hard ones again, and a doubtful note plays its bar slowly.

What you need

Fretscribe does not write tabs by itself. Choose your device to see what it does alone and what it needs the computer for.

{{figure platforms}}
  • A computer with Bandroom, on the same Wi-Fi as the phone: a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or 26, or a PC with Windows 11. Bandroom is free, and it writes every tab.
  • The downloads are not signed yet and are not in any app store. Each device needs a few extra steps the first time, and the download page says which.
  • Your recording never leaves your own network. It goes from your phone to your own computer and nowhere else. Bandroom's first run needs the internet, to download what it listens with.
