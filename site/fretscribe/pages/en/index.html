Turn a recording into tab.

Play a riff, record a song or open a file you have. Fretscribe writes it down as tab for your instrument and your tuning, shows you what it heard before a single fret is written, and lets you practise with the recording.

Read it, then play along.

The tab is yours to practise with, not just to look at.

  • The player plays your recording and follows the tab, bar and beat.
  • Speed slows the recording down, and Repeat bars plays the hard ones again and again.
  • Full screen leaves only the tab, for when the phone sits on an amp.
  • A note Fretscribe was not sure about gets a ?. Choose it to hear that bar slowly and decide for yourself.
  • The marks find some of the wrong notes, not most of them. A tab from a recording is a draft: trust your ears over it.

Written for your instrument.

The same notes sit in different places on a bass, a seven-string or a ukulele. Fretscribe asks once what you play and has every tab written for that.

  • Guitar, bass, ukulele and mandolin, with the number of strings you have.
  • Your usual tuning, and a capo when you use one.
  • Left-handed instruments, and instruments played upside down.
  • When a recording sounds like another tuning than yours, Check the song says so and offers it.

What you need

Fretscribe does not write tabs by itself:

  • A computer with Bandroom, on the same Wi-Fi as the phone: a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or newer, or a PC with Windows 11. Bandroom is free, and it writes every tab, for one instrument and for a full song.
  • The Fretscribe app on Android 16 or newer, iOS or iPadOS 26 or newer, macOS 26 or newer, or Windows 11.
  • Without the computer the app still opens the tabs you have made, to read and practise. It cannot write a new one.
  • The downloads are not signed yet and are not in any app store. Each device needs a few extra steps the first time, and the download page says which.

The recording goes from your phone to your own computer and nowhere else: no account, nothing online. Bandroom is the same program Brasscribe uses, so one computer serves both apps.
