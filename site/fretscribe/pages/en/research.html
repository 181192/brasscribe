Research

From a recording to tab

What Fretscribe does to a recording, how each step was measured, and where the tab is still wrong.

Fretscribe project · Every number and figure on this page is read from the repository's benchmark file when the site is built, so it changes when the file does · The same for brass-band scores: Brasscribe's paper

Abstract

In plain words: Fretscribe hears a bass line well and a guitar on its own fairly well. A guitar inside a full song is mostly beyond it. The frets it writes are playable, but often not the ones the player used, and its question marks catch a minority of the mistakes.

Fretscribe writes tablature for bass, guitar, ukulele and mandolin from a recording. Learned models find the beats and the notes, after separating the instrument from the mix when the recording is a whole song. Rules then clean the notes, put them on the beat grid, and choose a string and a fret for every note by the easiest path for the hand. No model was trained for this. Measured with a 50 ms tolerance, a bass scores onset F1 {{span fretted/bass-tab/song.onset_f1 fretted/bass-tab/instrument.onset_f1}} on {{data fretted/bass-tab/song.tracks int}} synthesized songs, and a real guitar played alone {{span fretted/guitar-tab/report.solo.onset_f1 fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.onset_f1}} on players the rules were not set on. A guitar inside a synthesized song scores {{data fretted/guitar-tab/song.onset_f1}}. Of the correctly heard notes of a single-line guitar part, {{data fretted/guitar-tab/report.solo.string_agreement pct}} are written on the string the player used, and {{data fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.string_agreement pct}} in chord playing. The tempo is right for {{span fretted/bass-tab/song.tempo_ok fretted/bass-tab/instrument.tempo_ok fretted/guitar-tab/report.solo.tempo_ok fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.tempo_ok fretted/guitar-tab/song.tempo_ok pct}} of the bass and guitar recordings. A doubt mark is on a wrong note {{span fretted/bass-tab/song.doubt_precision fretted/bass-tab/instrument.doubt_precision fretted/guitar-tab/report.solo.doubt_precision fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.doubt_precision fretted/guitar-tab/song.doubt_precision pct}} of the time, but only {{span fretted/bass-tab/song.doubt_recall fretted/bass-tab/instrument.doubt_recall fretted/guitar-tab/report.solo.doubt_recall fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.doubt_recall fretted/guitar-tab/song.doubt_recall pct}} of the wrong notes are marked. Every tab that was scored is playable as written. The evidence for ukulele and mandolin is a handful of synthesized passages. None of this has been measured on a recording made with a phone.

1 The problem

In shortA player hears a riff and wants to know where to put their fingers. The notes are half of the answer; the string and the fret are the other half, and a recording does not say them.

Tablature shows where a note is played: a line for each string and a fret number on it. Writing tab from sound has two problems on top of ordinary transcription. First, the same pitch can usually be played in several places on the neck, and nothing in a microphone recording says which one was used. Second, the instrument is rarely alone: in a song it sits under a voice, drums and other instruments, and a guitar plays chords whose notes ring over each other.

Fretscribe runs on the player's own devices, as Brasscribe does, and shares its listening models and its rules for rhythm. Every tab is written on the player's computer: the app on the phone records, sends the recording there and shows the tab, and carries no listening models. This paper covers what is specific to tab and reports the tab results.

2 The pipeline

In shortModels find the notes, rules choose where the fingers go. The tuning can be changed afterwards without listening again.

{{pipeline fret-pipeline}}

Which model does what

  • BS-RoFormer SW[4] separates a whole song into stems, and the instrument's stem is listened to. It has stems for bass and for guitar. It has none for a ukulele or a mandolin, which are read from the guitar stem. The step is left out when the recording is the instrument alone.
  • Beat This![3] finds the beats and downbeats.
  • Basic Pitch[1] finds the notes. SwiftF0[2] gives a second opinion; it follows one line, so its opinion counts only where one note sounds at a time.

From notes to a tab

Rules remove overtones that the note model heard as notes, drop notes the instrument cannot play, and, for a bass, check whether a line heard an octave too high was really played there. The notes then go on the beat grid with written lengths, by the same rules as in Brasscribe. Each note then gets a confidence between 0 and 1. It is set by rule, not by a fitted model as in Brasscribe: 0.5 plus half of the loudness Basic Pitch reports for the note. A note is put in doubt, and carries a ?question mark, when it is faint and, in a single line, SwiftF0 did not hear it at that pitch; or, in a chord, when it is faint, or rather faint and not heard again within a short time. The thresholds were set by hand.

The fingering is a search, not a model. Notes that start together are placed together, each on its own string, within what a hand can span. Every candidate has a cost: how far the fingers stretch, how high up the neck the hand sits, and how far the hand must move from the previous notes, measured in millimetres along the neck so that the same rule fits a bass and a ukulele. The cheapest path through the whole passage is the tab. The same notes always give the same tab, and a separate check confirms that every position can be played. Open strings and known chord shapes also enter the cost. A note that cannot be fingered together with the others is left out until the tab can be played, so that every tab is playable holds by construction: {{span fretted/guitar-tab/report.solo.unplayable_dropped fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.unplayable_dropped pct}} of the notes on GuitarSet are left out, and {{span fretted/ukulele-tab/ukulele_high_g_heldout_dev.instrument.unplayable_dropped fretted/ukulele-tab/ukulele_high_g_heldout_dev.song.unplayable_dropped fretted/ukulele-tab/ukulele_low_g_heldout_dev.instrument.unplayable_dropped fretted/ukulele-tab/ukulele_low_g_heldout_dev.song.unplayable_dropped fretted/ukulele-tab/ukulele_baritone_heldout_dev.instrument.unplayable_dropped fretted/ukulele-tab/ukulele_baritone_heldout_dev.song.unplayable_dropped fretted/mandolin-tab/mandolin_heldout_dev.instrument.unplayable_dropped fretted/mandolin-tab/mandolin_heldout_dev.song.unplayable_dropped pct}} on the rendered strummed passages for ukulele and mandolin.

Tuning and reporting apart. No model is trained. The rules have thresholds, and for guitar they were chosen on three of GuitarSet's six players and are quoted here for the other three, so a threshold does not meet the recordings it was chosen on. Section 5.6 shows what that separation is worth. For bass there is no such split: the Slakh set is development data, and one threshold of the octave check was chosen on the very set that scores it. The rendered passages have held-out groups, with exceptions that eval/README.md lists rule by rule.

What is missing. No real recording of a bass, a ukulele or a mandolin with its notes. No recording made with a phone. No tab suite runs in CI: all of them need data or models that are not in the repository, so they are run by hand on one computer.

4 Measures

In shortWas the note heard, is it on the string the player used, and does the tab warn where it is wrong? All measures run from 0 to 1; higher is better.

    {{part measure-onset}} {{part measure-string}} {{part measure-doubt}} {{part measure-metre}}

Also reported. Tempo: the share of recordings whose tab is within 4 % of the reference tempo. Chord recall and precision: the same as for notes, counted only over notes that start three or more together. Playable: a hard gate, not a score: a tab with a position that cannot be played fails the benchmark. All onset measures on this page use the 50 ms tolerance.

5 Results

In shortBass is the best case. A guitar alone is usable as a draft. A guitar in a song is not there yet. The frets are a suggestion.

5.2 String and fret

{{chart tab-strings}}

The tab is always playable, but it is not a record of what the guitarist did. In chords, where the hand has few choices, {{data fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.string_agreement pct}} of the right notes are on the player's string. On single lines it is {{data fretted/guitar-tab/report.solo.string_agreement pct}}: the search prefers the least movement, and players choose positions for tone, for the next phrase, or from habit. Counted against everything that was played, {{data fretted/guitar-tab/report.solo.string_recall pct}} of a single line's notes are both heard and on the player's string.

5.4 The doubt marks

{{chart tab-doubt}}

The marks point at real problems and leave most problems unmarked, as in Brasscribe. On a bass, {{span fretted/bass-tab/song.doubt_precision fretted/bass-tab/instrument.doubt_precision pct}} of the marked notes are wrong, and {{span fretted/bass-tab/song.doubt_recall fretted/bass-tab/instrument.doubt_recall pct}} of the wrong notes are marked. A tab without a ?question mark is not a checked tab.

5.5 A bass through a small microphone

{{chart tab-phone}}

People record with phones, and a small microphone passes little of a bass's lowest octave. This test removes the low end with a filter before anything listens. Up to a 200 Hz cut the line is still found, at a lower score. With a steeper cut at 300 Hz the score collapses and octave errors rise: {{data fretted/bass-tab/phone.hp300.octave_err_rate pct}} of the played notes are missed with a written note an octave or two away, against {{data fretted/bass-tab/phone.hp0.octave_err_rate pct}} with the full sound. The octave check moved {{data fretted/bass-tab/phone.hp300.line_moved pct}} of the lines whole at that cut, and none up to 200 Hz; that shift is the collapse.{{holds fretted/bass-tab/phone.hp200.line_moved <= 0}}{{holds fretted/bass-tab/phone.hp300.octave_err_rate > fretted/bass-tab/phone.hp200.octave_err_rate}} This is a filter on synthesized basses, not a phone in a room.

5.6 What the held-out players show

{{chart tab-heldout}}

Most measures hold up on the players the rules never saw. One does not: chord notes inside single-line playing. Without the split that number would have been quoted as {{data fretted/guitar-tab/tune.solo.chord_recall pct}}. 6 Where it fails

In shortAn instrument inside a song, the string a note is played on, and the bottom of a bass.

  • Inside a song. A guitar in a mix scores {{data fretted/guitar-tab/song.onset_f1}}, with {{data fretted/guitar-tab/song.chord_recall pct}} of its chord notes found. It is no higher where the song has a single guitar ({{data fretted/guitar-tab/song_one.onset_f1}}).{{holds fretted/guitar-tab/song_one.onset_f1 < 0.5}}
  • Strings. Fewer than half of a single line's right notes are on the player's string (5.2).
  • Octave errors are few on a bass in a song ({{data fretted/bass-tab/song.octave_err_rate pct}} of played notes) and grow at the bottom of a five-string ({{span fretted/bass-tab/low_b.song.octave_err_rate fretted/bass-tab/low_b.instrument.octave_err_rate pct}}) and when the low end is missing (5.5). On guitar chords the rate is {{data fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.octave_err_rate pct}}.
  • Chords. In chord playing {{data fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.chord_recall pct}} of the chord notes are found, and {{data fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.chord_precision pct}} of the chord notes written are right.
  • Tempo is wrong for up to four recordings in ten (5.3).
  • Tremolo. A mandolin melody picked in fast repeated notes scores {{span fretted/mandolin-tab/mandolin_tremolo.instrument.onset_f1 fretted/mandolin-tab/mandolin_heldout_tremolo.instrument.onset_f1}} alone, and {{data fretted/mandolin-tab/mandolin_tremolo.song.onset_f1}} and {{data fretted/mandolin-tab/mandolin_heldout_tremolo.song.onset_f1}} in a song, on one rendered passage each.
  • Notes removed in cleaning. The overtones and faint notes that the cleaning removes amount to {{span fretted/mandolin-tab/mandolin_tremolo.instrument.leftovers_dropped fretted/mandolin-tab/mandolin_tremolo.song.leftovers_dropped pct}} of the notes written in the tremolo passages and {{span fretted/guitar-tab/idmt.tune.leftovers_dropped fretted/guitar-tab/idmt.report.leftovers_dropped pct}} on IDMT-SMT-Guitar, against {{span fretted/guitar-tab/report.solo.leftovers_dropped fretted/guitar-tab/report.comp.leftovers_dropped pct}} on GuitarSet's held-out players.

7 Speed

In shortNot measured for tab.

A tab job runs the same models as Brasscribe: the separator for a whole song, Beat This!, Basic Pitch and SwiftF0. Brasscribe's paper gives the time and memory of each model on one laptop. No whole tab job has been timed, and nothing has been timed on a phone.

8 Limitations

In shortReal recordings for guitar only, nothing in CI, and nothing recorded with a phone.

  • Bass, ukulele and mandolin are measured on synthesized sound only. For ukulele and mandolin it is one sampled instrument each and a few passages, which the benchmark itself calls weak evidence.
  • No tab benchmark runs in CI. The numbers are gated when someone runs the benchmark by hand on a computer with the data.
  • Development data. Only the guitar rules have players that were kept apart (5.6).
  • No phone recordings. The microphone test is a filter (5.5).
  • String agreement is only known for guitar, because only the guitar sets say which string was played.
  • The doubt marks judge pitch and start only. They do not cover the string, the rhythm or the tempo.
  • Playing techniques such as bends, slides and hammer-ons are not scored by any benchmark here.
  • Common time. Nearly all recordings are in four; the result in other metres rests on {{data fretted/meter-tab/excerpts int}} rendered passages.

9 What is next

  • Real recordings of bass, ukulele and mandolin with their notes, and recordings made with phones.
  • The guitar in a song.
  • Strings a player would choose, measured against GuitarSet's strings.
  • A tab benchmark in CI on data that may be redistributed.
  • Timing of whole tab jobs, on a computer and on phones.
