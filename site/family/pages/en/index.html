A score for your band. A tab for your guitar.

Two apps that listen to a recording and write down what was played. Each is made for its own players; both keep your music on your own devices.

{{products}}

Your phone asks. Your computer listens.

Picking single notes out of a recording is heavy work, more than a phone should do on a bus. So the apps share one helper: Bandroom, a program on your own Mac or PC.

Bandroom on the computer
Does the listening and the writing: every band score and every tab. One Bandroom serves both apps and every phone you pair with it.
The app on your phone
Records, opens files, shows the score or the tab and plays along with you. Pair it once; after that it finds the computer by itself.
Private and local
The recording goes from your phone to your own computer and nowhere else. No account, no upload. Bandroom needs the internet once, to download what it listens with.
